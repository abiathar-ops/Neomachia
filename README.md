## Seliad

**Version 2.1 "Machia" · Crusader Kings III 1.20 "Crozier"**

---

### Overview
Seliad is a culture-and-religion mod. It adds a full culture with its own heritage, language and traditions, a religion with two rites, and a set of decisions, story cycles, artifacts and events. You begin as one of the children of Alnars, leader of a Misthophóroi mercenary band or a custom-character. The mod is designed to be as compatible as possible with other mods.
