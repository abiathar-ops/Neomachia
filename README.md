## Seliad

**Version 2.1 "Machia" · Crusader Kings III 1.20 "Crozier"**

---

### Overview
Seliad is a culture-and-religion mod. It adds a full culture with its own heritage, language and traditions, a religion with two rites, and a set of decisions, story cycles, artifacts and events. You begin as one of the children of Alnárs 'Sōtēr', a leader of the first Misthophóroi band to venture outside the confines of their mountainous sanctuaries of exile or either a landless or landed Selian custom-character. Seliad itself is created to be as compatible as possible with other mods.
